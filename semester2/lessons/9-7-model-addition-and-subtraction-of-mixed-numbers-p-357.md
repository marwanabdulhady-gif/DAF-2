### Solve & Share

**Look Back!** How can you estimate the sum?

### Visual Learning Bridge

Badr has boards 2¾ ft and 1⅚? (dump: 2 ³⁄₁₂ and 1 ⁸⁄₁₂ style). Total length? How much longer is one than the other?

**Add with fraction strips:** add fractional parts; rename if ≥ 1; add wholes; combine.

Example: 2⁵⁄₁₂ + 1⁸⁄₁₂  
Fractional parts: ⁵⁄₁₂ + ⁸⁄₁₂ = ¹³⁄₁₂ = 1 ¹⁄₁₂  
Wholes: 2 + 1 = 3  
3 + 1 ¹⁄₁₂ = **4 ¹⁄₁₂**

**Subtract on a number line:** mark 2¾; move 1⅚ left. Difference as a fraction.

**Convince Me!** Boards 2⅓ and 1⅔. Total? Difference?

### Another Example

Number line: 1⅓ + 2⅔. Start at 1⅓; move 1 right twice, then ⅔.  
Fraction strips: 2⅔ − 1⅔ — cross out one whole and two thirds.

### 🔘 Multiple Choice (IXL Style)

**Q1.** Which fraction is equivalent to 2/3?

   - A) 4/6
   - B) 3/4
   - C) 4/9
   - D) 2/6

<details>
<summary><b>✓ Show Answer</b></summary>

**Answer:** 4/6

**Explanation:** Multiply numerator and denominator by 2: (2×2)/(3×2) = 4/6.
</details>

### ✓✗ True or False (Quizizz Style)

**Q2.** True or False: 3/4 > 5/8

<details>
<summary><b>✓ Show Answer</b></summary>

**Answer:** True

**Explanation:** Convert to common denominator: 3/4 = 6/8. Since 6/8 > 5/8, then 3/4 > 5/8.
</details>

### ✏️ Fill in the Blank (Wordwall Style)

**Q3.** 2/5 + 1/5 = ___

<details>
<summary><b>✓ Show Answer</b></summary>

**Answer:** 3/5

**Explanation:** When adding fractions with the same denominator, add the numerators: (2+1)/5 = 3/5.
</details>

### 🎨 Visual Model Question

**Q4.** Draw a fraction bar to show that 1/2 = 2/4 = 4/8

<details>
<summary><b>✓ Show Answer</b></summary>

**Answer:** Visual

**Explanation:** Draw three bars of equal length. Divide first into 2 parts (shade 1), second into 4 parts (shade 2), third into 8 parts (shade 4). All show the same amount.
</details>
